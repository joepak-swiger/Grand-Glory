# Prototype gameplay

## Controls

- Move: WASD / arrow keys
- Dash: Shift or Space
- Prototype special: Q (earned through combat)
- Pause/resume: Escape
- Upgrade/menu selection: click or Enter

## Run and prototype balance

The standard run lasts about three minutes, with rising spawn pressure and an unnamed prototype Gang Leader at the end. Defeating the boss ends the run in victory. `?test=1` shortens the encounter and uses a fixed seed to support browser verification.

Blue Pinwheel's **Pinwheel (Prototype)** automatically throws rotating pinwheel-shaped geometric projectiles at the nearest enemy. The **Orbit Guard (Prototype)** unlocks from upgrades and deals short-range periodic damage. **Prototype Special — Canon TBD** is a temporary area attack. These are gameplay experiments, not canon.

Thug and Fast Thug enemies pursue and deal contact damage. The Gang Leader uses telegraphed attacks. Enemies drop XP, which is collected automatically nearby. A level-up pauses simulation until one of three upgrades is chosen.
